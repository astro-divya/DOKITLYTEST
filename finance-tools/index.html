Make assumptions visible before comparing financial numbers

Financial calculators produce estimates from assumptions, not advice or market guarantees. Start with the cash flow, interest convention, time period and whether fees, inflation or taxes are included.

Choose the right starting point

Financial tools can help with interest, instalments and returns, but the output depends entirely on the entered rate, period and payment schedule.

A practical example

When checking a loan, compare monthly instalment, total repayment and total interest. A lower instalment can still mean a larger total cost if the loan runs longer.

What to check before sharing the result

For investments, distinguish assumed annual returns from guaranteed payouts. SIP projections illustrate scenarios and should not be treated as forecasts of actual market performance.

Accuracy, quality and limitations

Taxes, charges and compounding conventions differ by product. Verify terms from the relevant institution and review whether inflation changes the real value of the result.
